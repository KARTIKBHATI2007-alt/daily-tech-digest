## arXiv Research

* **TeamGym: A Gym for Team Decision-Making by AI Agents with Distributed Information**  
  This paper introduces TeamGym to benchmark LLM agents on collaborative decisions requiring distributed information, finding that information distribution and retrieval hurdles lower task success rates by 22.9 and 19.9 percentage points, respectively. Training through reinforcement learning on final-decision loss improved decision quality and prompted emergent coordination among the agents.  
  [Source Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37571v1)

* **Gym-Anything-RT: Scaling Real-Time Environments for Vision-Language Agents**  
  Researchers developed an automated pipeline of 100 visual minigames to benchmark vision-language models in real-time interactive settings. The study identified substantial performance gaps, showing that an agent like GPT-6 Astra (xHigh) scored only 9.0% when acting in real time, compared to 75.0% when the environment was paused.  
  [Source Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37567v1)

* **The Structure Gap: When More Data Cannot Replace Structured Reasoning**  
  This study defines the "structure gap," demonstrating that data-driven learners operating without explicit governing structures face persistent uncertainty and excess risk compared to structured solvers on finite datasets. Controlled evaluations in linear inverse problems, sparse recovery, and graph reachability confirm that structure is critical for finite-data identification and extrapolation.  
  [Source Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37523v1)

* **Penny for Your Prompts: A Measurement Study of Contextual Leakage in AI Chat Platforms**  
  An audit of 42 web-based AI chatbot platforms revealed that third-party analytics and tracking scripts frequently capture sensitive user interactions. Across the examined services, 21 leaked chat conversation titles, nine leaked verbatim prompts, and multiple platforms transmitted user emails alongside conversation contexts.  
  [Source Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37467v1)

* **An EVM-Cosmos Bridge for Atomic, Semantic-Preserving Transfer of Environmental Tokens**  
  This paper details a cross-chain bridging protocol between EVM and Cosmos networks designed to preserve token semantics and ensure atomic transfers using extended hash time-locked contracts. The protocol was formally verified using TLC and validated on the Polygon and Neutron mainnets with median latencies under 100 seconds and minimal messaging overhead.  
  [Source Link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37663v1)
